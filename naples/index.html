Common Questions

FAQs for Naples Events

Do you serve Marco Island? +
Yes — we serve Marco Island resorts, estates, and private events regularly. The island is part of our standard Southwest Florida service area at no additional charge.
Can you set up at a Naples country club or private community? +
Absolutely. We work with private clubs and communities throughout Naples and provide a COI for any venue requirements. We coordinate directly with your club's event staff on logistics.
When is the best time to book during Naples season? +
Naples season runs October through April. These months fill up fast — especially November, December, and February. We recommend booking 6–8 weeks ahead for seasonal events and holiday parties.
Do you do charity golf events and fundraisers? +
Yes — charity events and galas are some of our most popular bookings in Naples. We can set up a closest-to-the-pin contest, a virtual tournament, or a standalone simulator station that guests pay to play, raising funds for your cause.
