Common Questions

FAQs for Ocala Events

Do you serve The Villages? +
Yes — The Villages is one of our favorite markets in Central Florida. We serve community events, clubhouse gatherings, private parties, and neighborhood events throughout The Villages complex.
Can you set up on a rural Ocala property or farm? +
Absolutely. Rural and estate setups are part of what we do in Marion County. We need a covered or indoor space of approximately 15x20 feet with power access. Contact us and we'll assess your specific property.
How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend 2–4 weeks for most events. For corporate holiday parties and large community events, 6–10 weeks is ideal. Ocala's fall and winter months (October–March) are our busiest season.
Do you do events for active adult and 55+ communities? +
Yes — active adult and 55+ community events are a perfect fit for our simulator. Golf is a natural draw, the competitive leaderboard aspect keeps it engaging, and the interface is simple enough for any skill level. Contact us to discuss your community event.
