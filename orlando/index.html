Common Questions

FAQs for Orlando Events

Do you serve Kissimmee and areas outside Orlando city limits? +
Yes — we serve the entire Greater Orlando metro including Kissimmee, Lake Nona, Winter Park, Dr. Phillips, Celebration, Windermere, and surrounding Central Florida communities. Contact us with your address to confirm coverage.
Can you set up at an Orlando hotel or convention center? +
Absolutely. We work with hotels, conference centers, and event venues regularly. We can provide a Certificate of Insurance for venue requirements and coordinate with your event coordinator on setup logistics.
How far in advance should I book for an Orlando event? +
We recommend booking 3–4 weeks in advance for standard events. For large corporate events, conventions, or peak season weekends (especially October–December for holiday parties), 6–10 weeks ahead is ideal. Orlando dates fill quickly during convention season.
What space do you need for setup? +
For indoor setup we need a minimum of 15×15 feet with 9–10 foot ceiling clearance. For outdoor setups, a covered or shaded area of similar size. We'll review your venue details during booking and confirm all requirements in advance.
Do you do corporate holiday parties in Orlando? +
Yes — Orlando corporate holiday parties are one of our most popular bookings October through December. The simulator works in any hotel ballroom or office space and turns a standard holiday party into a competitive, memorable experience. Book early — holiday weekends fill fast.
