FAQs for Miami Events

Do you serve all of Miami-Dade County? +
Yes — we cover all of Miami-Dade including Brickell, South Beach, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Kendall, Doral, Hialeah, and Aventura. Contact us with your address to confirm.
Can you set up at a Brickell or South Beach hotel? +
Absolutely. We work with Miami hotels and event venues regularly. We provide a COI and coordinate directly with your venue's event coordinator on setup requirements.
How far in advance should I book for a Miami event? +
We recommend 3–4 weeks for standard events. For corporate holiday parties and large events, 6–10 weeks ahead is ideal. Miami season (November–April) dates fill quickly.
Do you do corporate holiday parties in Miami? +
Yes — Miami corporate holiday parties are one of our most popular bookings October through December. The simulator works in any hotel ballroom or office space and creates a standout holiday event.
