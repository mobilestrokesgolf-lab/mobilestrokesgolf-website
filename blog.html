One of the most common questions we get is: "How much does a mobile golf simulator rental cost in Florida?" The honest answer is that it depends on a few key factors — but we believe in full transparency, so here's exactly what goes into the pricing and what you can expect at every level.

Mobile golf simulator rental in Florida typically ranges from around $500 for a shorter private event to $1,500+ for large corporate or full-day productions. Here's what drives that range.

What Factors Affect Golf Simulator Rental Cost?

  • Duration — How many hours of active play you need. More time = more staffing and wear on equipment.
  • Guest count — Larger groups need more management and may require extended sessions.
  • Location — Events further from our base in Broward County may include a travel fee for distant counties.
  • Event type — A corporate event with branded screens and a tournament structure requires more setup than a casual backyard party.
  • Add-ons — Physical mini-games (Putt-Putt, Chip Challenge), custom welcome screens, and Certificate of Insurance requests.

How to Plan a Golf-Themed Bachelor Party in South Florida

Planning a golf-themed bachelor party in South Florida that everyone actually talks about afterward takes more than a tee time. Between the heat, the pace of play, and keeping non-golfers entertained, a real course can fall flat fast. A mobile golf simulator solves every one of those problems — and delivers a more competitive, more social, and more memorable experience than 18 holes ever could.

Here's the complete playbook for pulling off the perfect golf bachelor party in South Florida, from the first planning call to the final round.

Step 1 — Choose Your Venue

The beauty of a mobile golf simulator is that you choose the location — the simulator comes to you. Popular bachelor party setups in South Florida include:

  • Private home or backyard — most popular for a relaxed, adults-only atmosphere
  • Rented vacation home or Airbnb — keeps the party contained and adds a premium feel
  • Private room at a bar or restaurant — combines food, drinks, and the simulator in one space
  • Hotel suite or rooftop terrace — ideal for destination bachelor weekends in Fort Lauderdale or Miami

For outdoor setups, you'll need a shaded or covered area — a tent, covered patio, or garage works perfectly. We'll confirm all space requirements during booking.

Step 2 — Pick Your Package

For bachelor parties, we typically recommend the Confident Strokes package — it includes leaderboard and tournament modes that are perfect for competitive group play, bragging rights, and keeping the energy high all night. For larger crews or all-day events, the Elite Strokes package adds physical mini-games like Putt-Putt and the Chip Challenge for even more variety.

Step 3 — Set Up the Tournament

One of the best features of our SkyTrak+ system is real-time leaderboard tracking. Here's a format that works perfectly for bachelor parties:

  1. Closest-to-the-Pin Contest — everyone gets one shot on a par 3, winner takes the round
  2. Longest Drive Competition — SkyTrak+ tracks ball speed and carry distance accurately
  3. 9-Hole Scramble — teams of 2–3, best ball counts, keeps the pace moving
  4. Minigame Finals — finish with a putting or chipping challenge for the crown

Our on-site caddy manages all of this — you just show up and swing.

Step 4 — Plan Around Non-Golfers

Not everyone in the bachelor party crew plays golf — and that's completely fine. Our simulator includes 10+ minigames designed for any skill level: zombie dodgeball, basketball shootout, and more. First-time swingers consistently end up the most competitive players of the night. Our caddy walks every guest through their first swing.

Pro tip: Book at least 3–4 weeks in advance for weekend dates in South Florida. Peak bachelor party season runs October through April and dates fill up fast — especially Fridays and Saturdays in Broward and Palm Beach counties.

South Florida Bachelor Party Locations We Serve

We cover the full stretch of South and Central Florida — Broward, Palm Beach, Collier, Lee, Sarasota, Hillsborough, Orange, Polk, Hardee, and Highlands counties. Whether the groom lives in Fort Lauderdale or you're flying everyone into Tampa for the weekend, we've got the county covered.

  • Works indoors or outdoors — rain doesn't cancel the party
  • Competitive leaderboard keeps the energy high all night
  • Non-golfers love it just as much as experienced players

Best Corporate Event Entertainment Ideas in South Florida (2026)

Planning corporate event entertainment in South Florida that actually lands — that people genuinely enjoy rather than just tolerate — is harder than it sounds. The usual options (catered lunch, keynote speaker, trust falls) rarely get people talking. The best corporate events create moments that stick long after the day ends.

Here are the most effective corporate entertainment ideas for South Florida events in 2026, ranked by engagement level and versatility — plus why a mobile golf simulator consistently outperforms every other option on this list.

1. Mobile Golf Simulator — The Crowd Favorite

A professional mobile golf simulator hits every mark for corporate entertainment: it's competitive without being physically demanding, inclusive of all skill levels, generates genuine conversation, and gives attendees something to do together rather than just stand around. It also photographs and videos beautifully — perfect for internal communications and social posts.

Mobile Strokes Golf brings a full SkyTrak+ launch monitor and BenQ 4K projection system directly to your corporate venue — a hotel ballroom, office atrium, outdoor terrace, or conference center. Our team handles every logistical detail. You run your agenda; we run the entertainment.

  • Real-time leaderboard creates friendly competition across departments
  • Works for groups of 10 to 500+ with proper scheduling
  • Custom welcome screen option for branded experiences
  • Certificate of Insurance available for venue requirements
  • No golf experience required — beginners often steal the show

2. Cooking Class or Chef Experience

A hands-on cooking experience with a local chef is a strong option for smaller groups (under 30). It's collaborative, memorable, and culminates in a shared meal. The downside: it doesn't scale well and requires a specific venue setup.

3. Escape Room (Private Group Booking)

Private escape room experiences are great for problem-solving team building — but they max out at 10–12 people per room, which means large corporate groups end up fractured. They also require traveling to a venue, which adds logistics.

4. Charity / Volunteer Component

Building a philanthropic element into your corporate event (backpack drives, meal packing, habitat builds) is highly effective for culture and morale. It works best as a morning activity before an afternoon entertainment component — paired well with a simulator or social activity.

5. Mixology or Cocktail Competition

A facilitated cocktail-making competition works well for evening events and creates a relaxed, social atmosphere. It pairs naturally with a golf simulator — guests rotate between mixing drinks and taking swings, which works brilliantly for 2–4 hour corporate socials.
