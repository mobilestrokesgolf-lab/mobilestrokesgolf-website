About Us

The Golf Experience Reimagined

At Mobile Strokes Golf, we believe golf should be more about good times than tee times. We bring a premium golf simulator experience directly to your event, making it easy for guests of all skill levels to jump in, compete, and have fun.

Whether it's a corporate event, backyard party, fundraiser, wedding, trade show, or a night with friends, our state-of-the-art simulator transforms almost any space into an interactive golfing experience. Play iconic courses, challenge your friends, and enjoy all the excitement of golf without the hassle of the course.

We're more than a golf simulator company — we're in the business of creating memorable experiences. With cutting-edge technology, exceptional service, and a fun, welcoming atmosphere, we make golf accessible and entertaining for everyone, from first-timers to seasoned players.

For an indoor setup, we typically need a minimum ceiling height of 9–10 feet and a footprint of approximately 15×15 feet. For outdoor setups in a shaded or covered area, requirements are similar. We'll review your venue details during the booking process and let you know exactly what's needed. For power, we can connect to standard outlets or work with a generator for outdoor events.

Yes — we accommodate both indoor and outdoor setups. Outdoors, we require a covered or shaded area for optimal screen visibility and equipment protection. During the booking process, you'll choose your preferred setup type, and we'll confirm any location-specific logistics.
We recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date, especially for weekends and peak season. For large corporate or festival events, 8–12 weeks ahead is ideal. Dates are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis.
A 50% deposit is required to confirm and hold your booking date. The remaining balance is due 7 days prior to your event. We accept major credit cards, Zelle, and check. Deposit terms for large-scale events may vary — contact us for details.
Our team typically arrives 60–90 minutes before your event start time to complete setup. Breakdown after the event takes a similar amount of time. Setup and breakdown are handled entirely by our staff — your guests only experience the finished result.
We monitor weather closely for all outdoor events. In the case of light rain with adequate cover, we can often continue. For severe weather or heavy rain, we may need to pause or reschedule. We'll work with you on any necessary changes to protect both your guests and our equipment. We strongly recommend a covered or indoor backup plan for outdoor events during Florida's rainy season.
